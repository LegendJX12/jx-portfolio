THE CHALLENGE

One in four people worldwide still lack safe drinking water. Conventional desalination is energy-hungry, depends on fossil fuels, and discharges brine that damages marine ecosystems.

THE SYSTEM
  1. Couples Forward Osmosis (FO), Pressure Retarded Osmosis (PRO) and Reverse Osmosis (RO) into one unified system.
  2. Recycles waste brine instead of discharging it.
  3. Extends membrane lifespan for higher efficiency and lower operating cost.
  4. Recovers more than 50% of energy, making plants more self-sufficient and resilient.
