# Brand & design system

## Concept
"Malaysian desktop wallpaper": full-bleed illustrated Malaysian scenes behind frosted-glass
panels styled like Windows 11 windows (rounded corners, title bar with — ☐ ✕).

## Scenes per section
- Home hero: Mt Kinabalu sunrise, rhinoceros hornbill, orangutan
- Experience: KL skyline at dusk (Twin Towers + KL Tower)
- Achievements (home): Pulau Redang beach, palms, sea turtle
- Education & credentials: George Town shophouses + Petaling Street lanterns
- Skills / languages / leadership: rainforest, Malayan tapir, Malayan tiger
- Contact footer: lion dance head on festive red
- Achievements page: Langkawi Sky Bridge at sunset, eagle
- Project page: Redang lagoon, turtle, reef fish
Scenes are SVG illustrations (in design-reference files). May later be swapped for my own photos.

## Colours (Jalur Gemilang)
- Navy (text, headings): #14205C
- Red (primary buttons, accents): #C8102E
- Yellow (award badges): #FFCC00
- Deep red (footer): #A10D24
- Ground: #F7F3EA · Body text: #13161B / #3A3F47 · Muted: #4A4F57
- Glass: rgba(255,255,255,0.74) + backdrop blur 18px, white 1px border, soft shadow

## Type
- Archivo (400–800) for headings and body
- IBM Plex Mono (400–500) for small labels, dates, captions

## Components
- Radius: 10px buttons, 14–18px cards/windows, 999px pills
- Buttons ≥ 48px tall; touch targets ≥ 44px
- Media badges: PPT red, PDF navy, CERT green #2B6E52, VIDEO purple #7A2E8E, IMG #4D6B3C
