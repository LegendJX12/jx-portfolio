Construction and demolition waste makes up about 20% of Singapore's waste, and 70% of demolition waste is crushed concrete. Team xyz proposed RECRE-Tech to turn that concrete into recycled concrete aggregates (RCA) for structural use, building on examples like the Samwoh Eco-Green Building, which uses 100% RCA.
