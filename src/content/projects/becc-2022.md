Under the competition's productivity pillar, we looked at why DfMA adoption is slow in Singapore: quality control at off-site factories, damage during transport, and inventory mistakes that cause double handling. Our Smart Construction System uses Internet of Things (IoT) technology to track components through manufacturing, delivery and assembly.
