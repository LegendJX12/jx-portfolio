Team U-2 designed **Med IT Ez**, a cabinet that stores medication securely and works with a cloud-connected app. It records each medicine and its schedule, reminds the user when to take it to avoid missed or double doses, and uses an AI motion-tracking camera to flag abnormal activity that could signal a health risk.
